## Research Gap Summary

Existing work has developed distributional embeddings, multilingual representation learning, morphology-aware modeling, cognate detection, and historical linguistic NLP largely as separate research strands. For Turkic languages, this leaves a gap: morphology, cognates, and historical lineage are rarely integrated into a single reproducible multilingual embedding framework that also supports retrieval and QA-oriented evaluation. The proposed work is motivated by this gap and frames linguistic knowledge as structured evidence for embedding construction and evaluation.
