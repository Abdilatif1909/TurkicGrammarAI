# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

TurkicGrammarAI is a research and engineering project. Contributors are expected to communicate respectfully, review evidence carefully, and keep discussions focused on improving the repository and its scientific reliability.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Prefer evidence from code, data, tests, or cited sources.
- Clearly label uncertainty, generated data, and unreviewed claims.
- Give actionable feedback in reviews.
- Respect linguistic, cultural, and regional diversity across Turkic-language communities.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Misrepresenting generated or unreviewed data as expert-validated.
- Publishing private data, credentials, or personal information.
- Repeatedly ignoring maintainer guidance on scope, safety, or scientific claims.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior harms the project or contributors. Concerns can be raised privately with repository maintainers.
