## Main Findings Summarized

- A 100,000-record embedding resource covering eight Turkic language varieties
- Morphological, cognate, and historical metadata integrated into FastText training
- Intrinsic Top-1, Top-5, and Top-10 results and positive-negative similarity separation
- Semantic-search Recall@1, Recall@5, Recall@10, and MRR
- RAG Recall@1, Recall@5, Recall@10, and MRR
- QA answer accuracy, source accuracy, and support coverage
- Stronger top-ten candidate recovery than first-rank selection
- Weaknesses in cross-language ranking, morphology QA, and global cognate clustering
- Explicit absence of comparable baseline results

## Future Work Items

- Expert-reviewed morphology, cognate, historical, and QA datasets

- Controlled removal of morphology, cognate, lineage, and language signals
- Evaluation of contextual multilingual and sentence-level embedding models
- Expanded Turkic language, dialect, script, and domain coverage
- Improved Uyghur normalization and historical-resource provenance
